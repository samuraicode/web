Push Your Luck

Supplies go in your haul, zombies grab at you. Stop and stash, or roll again for more. Three grabs and you run, dropping everything.

Every night you eat, and every night the horde grows. The longer you hold out, the redder the dice get.

Support

How do I learn to play?

Tap Tutorial on the start screen. It walks through a two-turn game, and anything else is explained the first time it comes up. Rules on the start screen shows every die face.

How do I play online?

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings, choose Multiplayer, then Find an opponent or Invite a friend. Both players need the latest version of the game.

My online game ended early.

A dropped connection can't be rejoined, so it counts as a forfeit. Leaving the app gives the other player a 30-second wait before the game is forfeited.

I bought a pack but didn't get my items.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then reopen the game. If the items still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.
