Support

My reminders aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are on for Randomly in your iPhone's Settings, under Notifications. In the app, make sure at least one library is on and that your active window in Settings covers the hours you're awake. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

Can I see exactly when the next one comes?

The Upcoming tab shows roughly when, morning, afternoon or evening, but keeps exact times hidden so reminders still feel unexpected. Shuffle in Upcoming picks a fresh set of times.

Why is one of my libraries paused?

The free version sends from two libraries at a time. Turn one off to switch, or unlock Randomly Plus to use as many as you like, along with libraries you write yourself.

I bought Randomly Plus but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Plus is shared with your Family Sharing group too.
