Support

How do we play together?

Lay the phone or iPad flat between you. Each player gets their own side of the screen, turned to face them. Pick a game, choose how many players, and press Play. To play alone, choose 1 player and pick how tough the CPU is.

How do I learn a game?

Every game's start page shows the rules in three pictures. Most games take one round to pick up.

What's free?

Tap Race, Reaction, Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four and Hot Potato are free forever, and every other game gives you one free match. One purchase unlocks every game, including new ones, and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought the unlock but games are still locked.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then tap a locked game and choose Restore purchase. If games still don't unlock, email us with the date of the purchase.
