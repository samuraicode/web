Recess Rumble Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Recess Rumble is a set of party games played on one device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it never connects to a server of ours. Everything stays on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Recess Rumble does not collect any information at all. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Your Apple Account details
  • Location data
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or game scores
  • Payment or card details

What Stays on Your Device

A few settings are saved on your device so the app remembers them: whether sound is on, the CPU difficulty you last chose, and which games you've used your free match on. They never leave your device, and deleting the app removes them.

In-App Purchase

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account by Apple, not to any account with us.

Children's Privacy

Recess Rumble is made to be played by families. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, shows no ads, and has no links out of the app except Apple's own purchase screens.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

App Store Privacy Label

In accordance with Apple's App Store requirements, Recess Rumble's privacy label is Data Not Collected.
