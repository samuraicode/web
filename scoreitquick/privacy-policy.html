ScoreItQuick Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At ScoreItQuick, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our app handles your information when you use our score tracking service.

Information We Do NOT Collect

ScoreItQuick does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports containing personal data
  • Any other personally identifiable information

How ScoreItQuick Works

ScoreItQuick operates entirely on your device:

  • Local Storage: Your games, players, and scores are stored locally on your device
  • No Account Required: The app does not require registration or account creation
  • No Data Transmission: Your game data never leaves your device
  • No Third-Party Services: The app does not connect to any external services

Data Security

Since ScoreItQuick does not collect personal information:

  • There is no personal data at risk of breach
  • Your game data remains private on your device
  • All app data is protected by your device's built-in security features

Children's Privacy

ScoreItQuick is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special privacy concerns for children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

App Store Privacy Labels

In accordance with Apple's App Store requirements, ScoreItQuick's privacy label indicates:

  • Data Not Collected: This app does not collect any data
  • Data Not Linked to You: No data is linked to your identity
  • Data Not Used to Track You: No data is used for tracking
